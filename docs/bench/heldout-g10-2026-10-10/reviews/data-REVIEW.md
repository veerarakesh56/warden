# G10 DATA held-out set: independent review

**Reviewer:** an independent reviewer agent (Claude Opus 5.5), 2026-10-10.
**Scope:** all 41 cases (`incidents/*.json`), `key.json` and `README.md`, read line by line. No file was changed except this one, and no AWS calls were made.
**WARDEN files read:** `aws_stack.py`, `aws_describe.py`, `resources.py`, `aws_backend.py`, `database.py`, `tools.py`, `evidence.py`, `quarantine.py` and `models.py`. The generator scripts were not opened.

**Counts:** 1 HIGH, 10 MED, 20 LOW.

## What checks out

These parts match the reader code exactly:

- **ALARM lines:** dimensions are sorted, and stat, period and threshold are in the reader's format.
- **CHANGE lines:** names come in sorted label-key order and stop at 3 names; `CHANGE none` is correct.
- **State and config lines:** the `STATE` field allowlists and naming (`Value=`, `CacheUsageLimits_ECPUPerSecond_Maximum=`) are right, and so are `CLUSTER`, `TABLE` and `EVENT aurora`.
- **Database reader lines:** `postgres stuck/long-running/blocked/connections held` lines are correct, including the `[reader] ` prefix, `user=?`/`app=(none)`/`client=local` for autovacuum, and the 10-line limits.
- **Lambda lines:** `CONFIG lambda` env masking is right (`DB_SECRET_ARN` and `KEY_LAYOUT` show as names only).
- **Metric keys:** `alarm_*` and `alarm_sibling_<ns>_*` keys, `ddb_*`, `aurora_db_load_<type>`, the `reader_*` prefix, and `:g` rendering are all right.
- **Metric math:** these alarms have no `alarm_*` metrics.
- **Tool errors:** every one has the exact `<sink>: <reader chain>: [<outcome> on <Op>] text` shape, with the right outcome classes. Examples: `ReadTimeoutError` gives `[timed out]` with no operation; `CacheClusterNotFound` gives `[not found on DescribeCacheClusters]`; a `ToolError` with no outcome gives `[failed (unclassified)]`; `PartialData` is handled.
- **`empty_reads`:** correct in all five cases that set it.
